Who we are and what the Cadet TV Personality Awards stands for.

Our Mission

The Cadet TV Personality Awards is an initiative by Cadet Media Ghana, aimed at discovering, nurturing, and celebrating young broadcasting talent in Ghana. The program provides a platform for students to develop and showcase their skills in journalism, news reporting, and media production.

How It Works

Students register under one of 15 nominee categories spanning international, local, sports and entertainment news, plus weather presenting. Each school may field one nominee per category. Once approved, nominees are assigned an official Nalo voting code and the public votes for their favorites via SMS/USSD. A panel of judges also scores performances, shown on our Judges' Ratings page.
